Services

Home cleaning · Recurring

The same people. A price that does not move.

Weekly, bi-weekly or monthly. The quoted price is the price on every visit — not a first-visit teaser.

Home cleaning · Deep clean

Deep clean: 82 checks, not a mood.

Baseboards, window tracks, inside oven and fridge. The difference from standard is a table, not a paragraph.

Home cleaning · Move-in / move-out

Move-out cleaning that matches the inspection.

Inside cabinets, oven, fridge, tracks and baseboards. Built for the landlord inspection, not a “sparkle” pass.

Drywall dust, paint specks, sticker residue and tracks. A standard recurring visit is not built for this.

Questions

What is included in a house cleaning?

Every room has a published checkpoint list. A standard visit is 53 checks; oven, fridge and inside cabinets are add-ons unless you book deep or move-out. See the room explorer.

Is the price hourly or flat?

Flat. The number you are quoted is the number you pay. If the home takes longer, that is our problem, not your invoice.

Do I get the same cleaners?

We schedule by area so you are not drawing from a rotating roster. Crew assignment is handled by us — you do not pick a named cleaner at booking.

Do I need to be home?

No. Access instructions and pet details are first-class fields in the booking form.
